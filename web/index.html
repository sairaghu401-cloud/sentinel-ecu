    UNDERSTANDING THE MODEL

    Reading the results

    Simulated time
    The model clock advances only when running or stepping. Changing speed accelerates this clock.
    Gateway latency
    Modeled CAN/gateway latency estimates are separate from measured host processing time. Neither is a hardware benchmark.
    Safety authority
    The gateway can request degradation. An independent safety task acknowledges and latches the state until eligible recovery.
    Detection metrics
    Precision, recall, and false positives use labelled ground truth solely for scoring, never as detector input.
    Validation scope
    Seeded synthetic scenarios demonstrate this model's behavior. They do not establish real-vehicle safety, certification, or cross-dataset performance.

    Bring your own CAN trace

    Use standard 11-bit identifiers and up to 8 payload bytes. Timestamps must increase or remain equal. Imports are normalized to begin at zero and evaluated against this laboratory's signal schema.

    CSV · timestamp in milliseconds
    timestamp_ms,id,data,label
0,0x120,00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00,normal
20,0x120,00 00 00 00 00 00 00 01,normal
    candump · timestamp in seconds
    (0.000000) can0 120#0000000000000000
(0.020000) can0 120#0000000000000001

    Labels are optional and unlabelled frames remain unknown. CAN-FD and extended identifiers are not supported. Imported arbitrary IDs may be blocked as unknown.
